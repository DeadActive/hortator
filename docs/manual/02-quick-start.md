# Quick start
<!--blurb: From power-on to a playing beat in a few minutes.-->

## Before you start

Install Hortator (see Install, update and recovery) or open the web simulator. At power-on you see the start screen (the HortatoR logo collapsing into a scan line), then the **TRACKS** page. If a project was saved before, the last one saved loads (`LOADED` in the top bar, stopped).

You do not need to remember button names yet: every section of the screen is reached by one button, and **HOME** always takes you back.

## Make a beat in eight steps

1. Press [[SEQ]]. The 16 white keys are now the **steps** of the selected track (the track number is in the top bar, T1 to T8).
2. Tap white keys to turn steps on and off. A lit key is an active step.
3. Press [[PLAY]]. The sequencer runs; the playhead moves along the keys.
4. Turn [[ALGORITHM]] to pick another track (T2, T3, ...) and tap in its steps.
5. Press [[EDIT]]. Turn [[KNOB 1]] to pick a different **model** for the track (kick, snare, hat, ACID bass...). [[KNOB 2]] to [[KNOB 4]] shape TUNE, DECAY, TONE.
6. Turn [[PRESETS]] to try the factory presets on the track, one per detent.
7. Hold a step and turn [[KNOB 1]] (PROB, chance), [[KNOB 2]] (RATCH, ratchet), [[KNOB 3]] (ACCENT) or [[KNOB 4]] (NUDGE, early or late) to give that step character.
8. Press [[SAVE]] and save the project into slot A to D. The last project saved loads at power-on.

## Ten things worth knowing in the first hour

- **Pages:** each button has several pages. Press it again, or turn [[SELECT]], to reach the next page. The footer shows `n/m`.
- **Tempo:** on HOME's TRACKS mode, [[SELECT]] sets the tempo. On any page, GLOBAL 1/7 [[KNOB 1]] does.
- **Bank:** a track has up to 64 steps. [[OCT+]] / [[OCT-]] move between banks of 16.
- **Mute and solo:** hold [[GLO]]: a white key mutes its track, a black key solos it.
- **Perform:** hold [[FX]]: the black keys are live effects (repeat, reverse, tape stop...), the white keys repeat a track's step, the knobs become FILTER / CRUSH / THROW / DEPTH.
- **Step tools:** hold [[SEQ]] for copy, paste, fill and duplicate on the steps.
- **Finger-drum:** tap [[REC]] on any page except TRACKS to arm all tracks, then play the white keys while playing. [[REC]] never starts the transport; [[PLAY]] does.
- **Play a melody:** tap [[SCL]] (shown as SEL on the simulator) for KEYS: the keys become a keyboard for the selected track, [[OCT-]] / [[OCT+]] its octave.
- **Lock a step:** tap [[ENV]] (the padlock appears), hold a step and turn a knob: that step plays its own value for that knob.
- **Sound menu:** hold [[HOME]] for the device menu (colour theme, backlight, USB audio, speaker EQ, about).
- **Panic:** hold [[SEQ]] while powering on for a *safe start* that loads nothing.
