Mouse and touch

  • Keys and buttons play while held, several at once.
  • Knobs: drag up or down, the mouse wheel, or hover and press ↑ ↓.
  • To hold a button (GLO, FX, SEQ + a key), right-click or long-press it; tap again to let go.

Getting around

  • SELECT turns the pages (on TRACKS: the tempo). ALGORITHM picks the track.
  • HOME switches TRACKS and HOME; hold it for the menu.
  • REC arms the selected track, on any page.
  • Hold GLO: the white keys mute the tracks.

Computer keyboard

  • Lower keys: Z X C V B N M , . / and S D F H J L ; '
  • Upper keys: Q W E R T Y and 3 4 6.
  • Space PLAY, Enter REC, Esc HOME, ← OCT−, → OCT+.

How to play it.

Every control, then ten things to do with them. Each works in the simulator above as on the FM‑1. The whole manual is in the README.

Point at a control, or at a line of the list.

Knobs

  • MASTER The volume: the speaker, the headphones and USB audio.
  • SELECT Turns a section's pages and moves the menu's cursor. On TRACKS: the tempo.
  • ALGORITHM Picks the track, on any page.
  • PRESETS Loads the next or the previous preset onto the track.
  • KNOB1 to KNOB4 Edit the four values on the screen.

Buttons

  • HOME TRACKS (levels, lengths, pans) and HOME (the scope, the track's first four knobs); a tap switches. Hold it: the menu.
  • EDIT The track's sound: its model and knobs. Hold it: the PRESETS list.
  • SEQ The steps on the white keys, then PATTERN, MOTION and SONG.
  • FX FILTER, FX, SLICER, RESON, delay, reverb, chorus. Hold it: PERFORM.
  • ARP Grids, a map of drum patterns.
  • LFO The track's two LFOs, then DEST: what they move.
  • ENV STEPS: the white keys are the track's steps on every page, for locks.
  • SEL KEYS: the keys play the selected track, a keyboard.
  • GLO A tap: GLOBAL (tempo, swing, clock, MIDI channel, sync, routing, thru, metronome). Hold it: the MUTE screen.
  • SAVE PROJECT (save, load and name slots A to D), then TOOLS.
  • PLAY Play and stop; on SONG, the song.
  • REC Arms tracks to record: on TRACKS the selected one, on any other page all of them.
  • OCT- OCT+ The next and previous 16 steps; the octave in KEYS; NO and YES, BACK and OK.

Keys

  • The 16 white keys: the steps, 1 to 16. On HOME, F3 to F4 play tracks 1 to 8; with GLO held they mute them.
  • The 11 black keys: with FX held, live effects on the whole mix. In KEYS, the sharps.

  1. Find your way

    Each section has its button: press it again, or turn SELECT, for its next page. ALGORITHM picks the track (T1 to T8 in the top bar), and the four knobs edit the four columns under it. HOME takes you back: TRACKS is the mixer, HOME the scope and the track's main knobs.

    SELECT pages, ALGORITHM the track, HOME back; hold HOME for the menu.

  2. Make a beat

    SEQ puts the track's steps on the white keys: tap one to turn it on or off, then PLAY. Hold a step and turn a knob for its chance, ratchet or accent. OCT+ and OCT- go to the next 16 steps, up to 64. PATTERN, the next page, sets the track's length, division and swing, or has it follow a Grids lane (SRC).

    SEQ, tap the white keys, PLAY. Hold a step: KNOB1 PROB, KNOB2 RATCH, KNOB3 ACCENT.

  3. Shape a sound

    EDIT opens the track's sound. KNOB1 picks the model, one of 45; TUNE, DECAY, TONE and a knob of the model's own follow, then its other knobs, the level, pan, note, choke group and PRIO on the next pages. PRESETS tries the factory presets on the track; hold EDIT for the list, to load one or save yours to the USER bank.

    EDIT, KNOB1 the model; PRESETS the next preset; hold EDIT for the list.

  4. Play and record live

    SEL turns the keys into a keyboard for the track (KEYS), OCT- and OCT+ its octave. REC arms the track on TRACKS; on any other page it is REC ALL, each white track key playing and recording its own track, to finger-drum a kit. REC never starts the transport: PLAY does. An armed track records knob turns too, as MOTION. On the STEP page, stopped and in KEYS, REC starts step record, a note at a time.

    SEL KEYS, REC to arm, PLAY to go.

  5. Mute tracks

    Hold GLO: the MUTE screen. The white keys F3 to F4 mute and unmute tracks 1 to 8 (a filled box is heard, an outline muted). The top C# makes a mute land at once, the top D# on the next bar: made while playing, it blinks until the downbeat. Let go of GLO to go back.

    Hold GLO, press the white keys.

  6. Perform

    Hold FX: the black keys play effects on the whole mix while held: repeat 1/8, 1/16 and 1/32, reverse, tape stop, filter sweeps, freeze, an octave up or down. The white keys silence their tracks while held. The knobs become FILTER, CRUSH, THROW and DEPTH, which stay on when you let go, an FX in the top bar, until turned back to 0.

    Hold FX: the black keys, the white keys, KNOB1 to KNOB4.

  7. Lock a step

    Tap ENV (lit, a padlock in the top bar): the white keys are the track's steps on every page. Hold a step and turn a knob, on HOME, EDIT, FX or LFO: that step plays its own value, and the next one the knob's again. Hold the step and tap OCT- to clear its locks on that page; hold OCT- for 0.6 s to clear all of them.

    ENV, hold a step, turn a knob; OCT- clears.

  8. Save and chain

    SAVE opens PROJECT: four slots, A to D, in the FM‑1's flash, each with a name. The last project saved loads at power-on; with SEQ held at power-on nothing loads. SONG, SEQ's last page, chains up to 16 rows of projects, each with its repeats. TOOLS clears a track's steps or its sound, or starts everything over.

    SAVE; SELECT to TOOLS. SEQ, SELECT to SONG.

  9. MIDI, USB audio and LEARN

    MIDI comes in over USB and the TRS jack: notes on the drum channel (CH, 10 at first) play the tracks whose NOTE matches (ROUT NOTE; SEL plays the selected track and TRACK channels 1 to 8 the eight tracks, pitched), and CLK follows a clock from either. SYNC sends a MIDI clock out over USB for a DAW or AUM to follow, and THRU passes the TRS jack's notes on to USB. MIDI LEARN ties up to 32 controls of a controller to knobs, PLAY, REC or PERFORM: hold GLO and press G4, turn a knob (or press PLAY), then move the controller's knob.

    Over USB the FM‑1 is also an audio interface, Hortator, that records into a computer. USB AUDIO in the menu sends 2 CH, the stereo master; 10 CH MIX, the master and the eight tracks each on its own; or 10 CH FX, the eight tracks dry and the send effects, to mix in your DAW. 10 CH needs the standard version, not Bluetooth; macOS and iPadOS take it, Windows may not.

    Tap GLO for CH and CLK. Hold GLO + G4 to learn, OCT+ when done. Hold HOME for USB AUDIO.

  10. Bluetooth

    The Bluetooth version adds a BLUETOOTH page to the menu. ROLE HOST: the FM‑1 connects a BLE‑MIDI controller, the M‑VAVE SMC‑Mixer or another FM‑1 itself; set BLUETOOTH ON, + ADD A DEVICE, pick it. ROLE DEVICE: the FM‑1 is Felucca FM‑1, a Bluetooth MIDI port for a Mac (Audio MIDI Setup, Bluetooth, Connect), an iPad app or another FM‑1. Notes, MIDI LEARN, CLK BLE and SYNC BLE work as over USB. FM‑Link keeps two FM‑1s in time: the leader on CLK INT (or following a DAW's clock), the follower on CLK LINK; PLAY on the leader starts both.

    It leaves out USB AUDIO 10 CH (USB audio is 2 CH there). Known issue: a saved device that is not connected can make the sound stutter now and then; FORGET the ones you don't use (KNOB 1 left on its line, OCT+).

    Hold HOME, SELECT to BLUETOOTH, OCT+. Step by step: the README.

  11. Make it yours

    Hold HOME for the menu. COLOR picks one of six screen themes, ACCEL speeds a knob up as it turns faster, BACKLIGHT lets the unlit keys glow to find them in the dark, SPEAKER EQ adds bass to the small speaker. METRO, on GLOBAL's last page, clicks every quarter note. The Bluetooth version adds BLUETOOTH to the menu.

    Hold HOME, SELECT to a row, OCT+ OK, OCT- back.

Hear what it does.

Every picture is drawn by the firmware's own sound code. Turn the knobs; press Play.

45 engines in three banks

MD has 21, measured from the Machinedrum: 13 TRX and 8 EFM. MODERN has 10, among them ACID, a 303-style bass. CLASSIC has 14, the 808 and 909 family. 151 factory presets and 128 user slots.

EDIT, then KNOB1 picks the model; on the HOME screen (HOME) four knobs shape it.

Resonators

Any track can ring through a string, pipe, chord or modal bar, tuned in semitones. Grey is the dry hit, bright the ring.

FX, then SELECT to RESON.

A compressor that pumps

The Streams compressor, keyed by the kick (here BDTRX), ducks the rest: ACID's bass line and the hats pump. Amber is the gain reduction. GHOST keeps the pump with the kick muted.

HOME to the HOME screen, SELECT to COMP; a white key ducks a track.

Two LFOs on every track

Ten waves with a morph, synced to the tempo, each on its own destination. Here they move a hi-hat's MTAL and decay.

LFO: LFO 1 on KNOB1-KNOB2, LFO 2 on KNOB3-KNOB4; DEST is the last page.

Steps that roll dice

A step can play by chance, ratchet, or wait for a fill bar. Mutes can wait for the next bar, and knob moves record into the pattern. Parameter locks give one step its own value for a knob, on any sound page: the next step plays the knob again. Each slot has 8 variations; queue the next slot or variation and it comes in at the pattern's end. Steps set to FILL play only while you hold a fill. COPY and PASTE a whole slot.

SEQ: hold a step, KNOB1 chance, KNOB2 ratchet. Hold GLO for mutes; REC records knob moves. ENV: the keys are the steps on every page; hold one and turn a knob to lock it, OCT- clears. The black keys queue a variation on HOME and SEQ; hold SAVE: white keys 1–4 queue a slot. Hold REC off SEQ: FILL. SAVE's third page: COPY / PASTE.

Grids

Mutable Instruments' map of drum patterns: move through it and the kick, snare and hat lanes rewrite. Three densities; any track can follow a lane.

ARP; SELECT to the fills.

Effects, per track and live

Each track has a filter, resonator, distortion and slicer, then delay, reverb and chorus. Hold FX and the black keys repeat, reverse, filter, stop or freeze the mix.

FX for the pages; hold FX and play the black keys.

Bluetooth MIDI

The Bluetooth version connects to Bluetooth MIDI controllers itself. An M-VAVE SMC-Mixer becomes its mixer (levels, the selected track's knobs, MUTE, SOLO); other controllers play the tracks. It is also a Bluetooth MIDI device for a computer or a tablet, and FM-Link plays two FM-1s in time without a cable.

Hold HOME for the menu, SELECT to BLUETOOTH, OCT+. Off at power-on unless AUTOSTART is ON.

Put it on your FM-1.

From a web page, over the FM-1's own USB cable, in a few minutes.

Install

  1. Open the installer in Chrome or Edge

    On a computer: only they allow USB MIDI.

  2. Plug in the FM-1

    A USB data cable, straight into the computer.

  3. Press Install and wait

    Keep the cable in until Done. The FM-1 restarts with Hortator.

    Open the installer

    Beta firmware, installed at your own risk: the author accepts no responsibility if your FM-1 is damaged or misbehaves.

    Back to the official firmware

    M-VAVE's updater, M-UPGRADE, puts it back: from m-vave.com/download, Downloads → PC Software → M-UPGRADE, with PC Firmware → FM-1 V15.

    Replug and press Install again: the FM-1 stays in update mode until a write has finished. If it no longer starts, recovery needs a Transporter.
