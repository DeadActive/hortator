Mouse and touch

  • Keys and buttons play while held. Several fingers work.
  • Turn a knob by dragging it up or down, with the mouse wheel, or by hovering it and pressing ↑ ↓.
  • To hold a button for a combination (SEQ held + a key), right-click it or long-press it. Tap it again to let go.

Computer keyboard

  • Lower keys: Z X C V B N M , . / with S D F H J L ; ' for the raised ones.
  • Upper keys: Q W E R T Y with 3 4 6.
  • Space PLAY, Enter REC, Esc HOME, ← OCT−, → OCT+.

What it does

Eight drum tracks

Each track plays a drum model: 808 and 909 kicks, snares and claps, hats, toms, cowbell, cymbal, rim, clave, conga, and Plaits-style kicks, snares and hats. Every model has TUNE, DECAY, TONE and a fourth knob.

On the panel: EDIT. ALGORITHM or a white key on HOME picks the track.

Step sequencer

Up to 64 steps per track, each track with its own length. Accents, swing, PROB and RATCH per step, fills that play every other bar, live recording, and mutes that wait for the next bar.

On the panel: SEQ, the 16 white keys are the steps. REC for TRACKS and recording.

Grids

Mutable Instruments' pattern generator: move through its drum map, add chaos, or switch to Euclidean rhythms. Any track can follow one of its three channels.

On the panel: ARP.

Compressor

The Streams compressor, keyed by any track: duck the hats and the reverb under the kick for a pumping mix. GHOST keeps the pump going with the key track muted.

On the panel: HOME, then PRESETS to the COMP pages.

LFOs and RESON

Two LFOs per track, ten waves with morph, synced to the tempo. RESON adds a string, pipe or chord resonator to a track.

On the panel: LFO; RESON on the FX pages.

Effects

Distortion and the SLICER on every track; delay, ROOM or SPRING reverb and chorus sends; a master limiter, and BASS+ to make the small speaker sound bigger.

On the panel: FX, PRESETS turns the pages. BASS+ is in the menu (hold HOME).

Put it on your FM-1

  1. Use Chrome or Edge on a computer

    The installer talks to the FM-1 over USB MIDI, which only these browsers allow.

  2. Connect the FM-1 with a USB data cable

    Straight into the computer. No probe or Transporter is needed.

  3. Press Install and wait

    A few minutes. Keep the cable in until it says Done; the FM-1 restarts by itself.

FM-1 Drums is beta firmware and may be unstable. You install it at your own risk: the author accepts no responsibility if your FM-1 is damaged or misbehaves. M-VAVE's own updater, M-UPGRADE, takes you back to the official firmware.
