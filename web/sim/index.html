Mouse and touch

  • Keys and buttons play while held. Several fingers work.
  • Turn a knob by dragging it up or down, with the mouse wheel, or by hovering it and pressing ↑ ↓.
  • To hold a button (GLO for the mutes, FX for PERFORM, SEQ + a key), right-click it or long-press it. Tap it again to let go.

Getting around

  • SELECT turns the pages; on TRACKS it sets the tempo. ALGORITHM picks the track.
  • HOME switches between TRACKS and the HOME screen; hold it for the menu.
  • REC arms the selected track for recording, on any page.
  • Hold GLO for the MUTE screen: the white keys mute and unmute the tracks.

Computer keyboard

  • Lower keys: Z X C V B N M , . / with S D F H J L ; ' for the raised ones.
  • Upper keys: Q W E R T Y with 3 4 6.
  • Space PLAY, Enter REC (arms the track), Esc HOME (TRACKS / HOME), ← OCT−, → OCT+.

Hear what it does.

Every picture here is drawn by the firmware's own sound code, running in your browser. Turn the knobs to change it; press Play to hear it.

Drum voices

Twenty-three drum models: the 808 and 909, FM, a modelled drumhead, Plaits-style kicks, snares and hats, and samples. On the FM-1 they are joined by ACID, a 303-style bass voice.

Each model has its own knobs. The picture is one hit, drawn as it sounds and as its spectrum.

On the panel: EDIT, then KNOB1 picks the model; on the HOME screen (HOME, tapped again from TRACKS) the four knobs shape it.

Resonators

Any track can ring through a string, a pipe, a chord or a modal bar, tuned in semitones.

A rimshot becomes a pluck, a snare a bell: the dry hit is the grey trace, the ring the bright one.

On the panel: FX, then SELECT to the RESON pages.

A compressor that pumps

The Streams compressor, keyed by the kick, pulls the rest of the mix down on every hit.

The amber trace is its gain reduction as it happens. GHOST keeps the pump going with the kick muted.

On the panel: HOME to the HOME screen (a tap switches from TRACKS), then SELECT to the COMP pages; a white key ducks a track.

Two LFOs on every track

Ten waves with a morph between them, synced to the tempo, each on its own destination.

Here they move a hi-hat: the first its tone, the second its decay, step by step.

On the panel: LFO; OCT- switches to the second one.

Steps that roll dice

A step can play with a chance, roll into a ratchet, or wait for a fill bar.

Mutes can wait for the next bar, so a drop lands on the one, and knob moves record into the pattern.

On the panel: SEQ: hold a step key and turn KNOB1 for the chance, KNOB2 for the ratchet. Hold GLO for the mutes; REC arms the track, on any page, to record knob moves.

Grids

Mutable Instruments' map of drum patterns: drag through it and the kick, snare and hat lanes rewrite themselves.

Three densities thin each lane out or fill it in. Any track can follow one of the lanes.

On the panel: ARP; SELECT to the fills.

Effects, per track and live

Every track runs through its own filter, resonator, distortion and slicer, then into delay, reverb and chorus.

Hold FX and the black keys repeat, reverse, filter, stop or freeze the whole mix while it plays.

On the panel: FX for the pages; hold FX and play the black keys to perform.

Bluetooth MIDI

The Bluetooth version of Hortator lets the FM-1 find Bluetooth MIDI controllers and connect to them itself, with no computer in between.

An M-VAVE SMC-Mixer becomes its mixer: the faders are the track levels, the knobs the selected track's first four, with MUTE and SOLO per track. Any other controller plays the tracks with its notes.

On the panel: hold HOME for the menu, SELECT to BLUETOOTH, then OCT+. It is off at every power-on unless its AUTOSTART is ON.

Put it on your FM-1.

It installs from a web page over the FM-1's own USB cable, in a few minutes. Nothing to download, no extra hardware.

Install

  1. Open the installer in Chrome or Edge

    On a computer: the installer talks to the FM-1 over USB MIDI, which only these browsers allow.

  2. Plug in the FM-1

    With a USB data cable, straight into the computer. No probe or Transporter.

  3. Press Install and wait

    Keep the cable in until it says Done. The FM-1 restarts with Hortator.

What's new

    Open the installer

    Hortator is beta firmware and may be unstable. You install it at your own risk: the author accepts no responsibility if your FM-1 is damaged or misbehaves.

    Back to the official firmware

    M-VAVE's own updater, M-UPGRADE, puts the official firmware back: from m-vave.com/download, Downloads → PC Software → M-UPGRADE, with PC Firmware → FM-1 V15.

    If an install fails

    Replug the cable and press Install again: the FM-1 stays in update mode until a write has finished. If it no longer starts at all, recovering it needs a Transporter.
